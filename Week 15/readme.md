### 2. Executive Dashboard Queries
Generated high-level business reports from the unified view:
- **Executive Summary:** Total platform revenue, total orders, and active customers.
- **Top 5 Best-Selling Products:** Aggregated by units sold and revenue.
- **Payment Gateway Performance:** Calculated the success rate of different payment modes (UPI, Credit Card, etc.).
